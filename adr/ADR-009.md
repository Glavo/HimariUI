# ADR-009: Lay out in logical pixels

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-14
- **Decision milestone:** ADR-BOOTSTRAP-001
- **Evidence:** PLANS.md Sections 4 (ADR-009), 8, and 26; scaling, scrolling, and long-list precision fixtures.
- **Supersedes:** None
- **Superseded by:** None

## Context

Device-pixel layout couples component geometry to display scale, while unbounded `float` accumulation eventually loses the precision needed for stable scrolling.

## Decision

Local layout coordinates and sizes use `float` logical pixels. DPI conversion, fractional scale, subpixel placement, and pixel snapping occur during layer compilation and rendering. Unbounded scroll and cumulative coordinates use anchor-relative computation or double-precision accumulation.

## Consequences

Public geometry remains compact and display-independent without treating `float` as sufficient for long-running sums. Hit testing, semantics, painting, and snapping consume the same resolved transforms.

## Evidence

M2 scaling and virtual-list fixtures cover fractional scale, anchor preservation, large cumulative offsets, and deterministic Headless behavior.

## Replacement

None.
